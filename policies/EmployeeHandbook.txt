Employee Handbook & HR Policy
1. General Code of Conduct
* Maintain a respectful, inclusive, and professional work environment at all times.
* Zero tolerance applies to any form of discrimination, bullying, or harassment based on race, gender, religion, age, or sexual orientation.
* Adhere to a professional dress code appropriate for your specific role and client interactions.
* Use company equipment, internet, and communication channels responsibly and primarily for business purposes.
2. Work Hours & Attendance
* Standard operating hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
* Notify your direct manager at least 2 hours before your shift begins if you anticipate being late or absent.
* Ensure accurate and timely logging of work hours, breaks, and task progress using the designated company software.
* Remote or hybrid work arrangements must be pre-approved by your department head.
3. Leave & Time Off
* Paid Time Off (PTO): Employees accrue 20 days of PTO annually. Requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance for approval.
* Sick Leave: Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
* Public Holidays: The company observes national and regional holidays according to the official annual calendar distributed by HR.
* Unpaid Leave: May be granted at management’s discretion for personal emergencies after all PTO is exhausted.
4. Confidentiality & Data Security
* Protect all proprietary company information, client data, intellectual property, and internal communications.
* Never share or forward sensitive company data to personal email accounts, unauthorized devices, or public networks.
* Report any lost devices, suspicious emails, or potential security breaches to the IT department immediately.
5. Performance & Reviews
* Employees will undergo formal performance evaluations annually.
* Promotions, raises, and bonuses are merit-based and tied directly to performance metrics and company profitability.
* Management is committed to providing regular, constructive feedback to support professional growth.
6. Disciplinary Action & Termination
* Violations of company policy, repeated absenteeism, or poor performance will follow a progressive disciplinary process (verbal warning, written warning, final warning).
* Severe infractions (e.g., theft, harassment, breach of confidentiality) may result in immediate termination without prior notice.
* A standard notice period of 30 days is required for voluntary resignation.
* All company-issued assets (laptops, keys, ID cards) must be returned on or before the final working day.
